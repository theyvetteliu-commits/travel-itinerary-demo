Kyoto stay  ·  Option 1
Option 1 · Traditional

A ryokan in Higashiyama

Steps from Yasaka Shrine, on a quiet stone lane

Twelve rooms, tatami and futon, a kaiseki dinner served in your suite on two of the nights. It is the more memorable stay and puts you in walking distance of the temples at first light. The trade-off is space: one large suite for four, and futons rather than beds.
